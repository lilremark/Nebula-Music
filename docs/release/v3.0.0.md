## Nebula 3.0

Nebula 3.0 brings the beta series to the stable channel. These are the major
changes since **2.4.6**, the previous stable release.

### A redesigned interface

- OLED-black and light themes, a collapsible navigation sidebar, and layouts
  that adapt to smaller windows and mobile screens.
- Larger album artwork, clearer library controls, and album/playlist headers
  that stay visible and shrink as you scroll through the track list.
- Refined search and Settings organized into keyboard-accessible sections.

### More ways to control playback

- A floating playback dock and responsive Now Playing sidebar, with smooth
  switching between layouts while music keeps playing.
- A refreshed always-on-top desktop mini player with more room for track
  details, elapsed/total time, and the upcoming queue.
- More detailed, seekable stereo waveforms and artwork-colored progress bars.
- Shared speed and pitch controls across player layouts, with independent
  pitch adjustment.

### Better Home recommendations

Home now shows up to **12 Quick Picks**. Your Rotation sits beside them on
wide screens, with **six-track pages** for your Top 50 and For You picks.
Browse every page without getting caught in a nested track scrollbar.

### Experimental local AI DJ on Windows

Start personalized sets from Discover, choose familiar, balanced, or discovery
listening, and hear local commentary in the Michael or Heart voice after
four or five completed tracks. Choose interludes between sets or speech over
lowered music, and enable optional transcription in Discover.

SmolLM3 and Kokoro models are optional, verified downloads from AI DJ Settings;
they are excluded from the installer. No session starts until you explicitly
start it. **AI DJ is Windows-only and is disabled and hidden on web and macOS.**

### Playback reliability, audio, and security

- Long queues and crossfade handoffs recover more reliably. Stale waveform
  downloads and unused crossfade streams no longer accumulate connections.
- AutoEQ preamp applies consistently to music, crossfades, and radio, including
  restored profiles and smooth live adjustments.
- Better account-specific library caches, artwork loading, and settings
  persistence, plus stronger desktop IPC and network protections.
- Updated dependencies with zero known findings in the full npm audit at
  release verification. See the [focused security review](https://github.com/lilremark/Nebula-Music/blob/main/docs/security-review-2026-10-09.md)
  for scope and limitations.

### Desktop downloads and updates

- **Windows x64:** `Nebula-3.0.0-setup.exe` or the AppX package. Taskbar thumbnail
  Previous, Play/Pause, and Next controls now register reliably and stay in sync.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** `Nebula-3.0.0-arm64.dmg` or the ZIP bundle.
- Choose Stable or Beta updates in Settings. Stable users receive published
  stable releases; the Beta channel also offers prereleases.

Both builds are unsigned, and macOS is not notarized. Windows may show
SmartScreen warnings; macOS may block first launch with Gatekeeper. Windows
updates install automatically; macOS updates open the release for manual DMG
installation.
