## AI DJ collection view

AI DJ in Discover now shares the Album and Playlist header, controls and track-row
styling, with responsive layouts for compact windows. The updated README includes
a screenshot of the new presentation.

The Discover cover keeps moving while visible, including idle and paused sessions,
and still responds to DJ speech. Reduced motion and unavailable WebGPU use a
static cover; other players continue to freeze their speech cover when paused.

Local AI DJ models remain an optional 2.01 GB download in Settings. Previously
downloaded models remain available after updating. Windows x64 and 16 GB RAM are
recommended; CPU inference requires no dedicated GPU. Allow 5 GB free disk space
for initial model installation (7 GB for repair).

See [AI DJ behavior and requirements](https://github.com/lilremark/Nebula-Music/blob/beta/docs/ai-dj.md).
