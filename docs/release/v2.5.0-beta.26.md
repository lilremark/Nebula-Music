## Player transitions and search focus

Switching between the bottom and sidebar players now eases the players and
content into place, with a proper closing animation. Playback continues
throughout the switch. Outgoing controls are disabled, keyboard focus moves to
the corresponding player control, and reduced motion uses an immediate change.

The search modal now has a rounded focus highlight contained within the input.
The input can shrink in compact layouts while the close button stays visible.

AI DJ models remain optional downloads in Settings. Previously downloaded models
remain available after updating.
