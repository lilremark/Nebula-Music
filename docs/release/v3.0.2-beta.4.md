This beta replaces the sign-in cover flow with an interactive quantum nebula
that reacts to your cursor and adapts to your theme's primary colour.

## Features

- Replace the sign-in screen's album cover flow with an interactive glowing particle nebula that softly fades into the sign-in panel.
- Interact directly with the nebula on the sign-in screen: move your pointer to ripple particles away, press and hold to pull them into a swirling vortex, and release to send a shockwave across the field.
- Tint the nebula automatically from your active theme's primary colour.
  - Falls back cleanly when WebGL2 is unavailable, pauses while the window is hidden, and stays still when your system prefers reduced motion.
